## License Safety Guide

| License | Commercial Use | Risk Level |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **CC0** | ✅ | Zero risk — public domain |
| **CC-BY** | ✅ | Low — just credit creator |
| **MIT/Unlicense** | ✅ | Zero risk |
| **CC-BY-SA** | ✅ | Medium — viral share-alike |
| **CC-BY-NC** | ❌ | **CANNOT USE COMMERCIALLY** |

**Golden Rule:** When in doubt, use CC0 assets only. Zero legal risk, zero attribution obligation.
